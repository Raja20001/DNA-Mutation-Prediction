SLIDE 2 :: INTRODUCTION

Introduction: Computational Genomics & Quantum AI

🧬 Project Domain & Context

Focuses on Bio-Quantum Informatics and Genomic Machine Learning. Analyzes high-throughput Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) data across 3.2 billion base pairs to identify functional single nucleotide variants (SNVs) and indels.

🌍 Clinical Importance & Motivation

Single nucleotide mutations in key oncogenes (TP53, BRCA1, EGFR) trigger malignant cellular transformation. Classical ML algorithms suffer from severe motif sparsity and fail to resolve non-Euclidean epistatic interactions.

⚡ Technological Context

Exploits Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum (NISQ) Hilbert-space embeddings combined with PyTorch dilated causal temporal convolutions to parse genomic sequences without future information bleed.

💡 Proposed Solution Overview

Formulates the Quantum Multi-Modal Fusion Network (QMFN) with zero-leakage chromosome isolation and live NCBI ClinVar clinical evidence integration for evidence-grounded interpretation.

SLIDE 3 :: PROBLEM STATEMENT

Problem Statement & Research Challenges

⚠️ Real-World Genomic Bottlenecks

  • VUS Diagnostic Crisis: Over 40% of discovered NGS variants remain Variants of Uncertain Significance (VUS), impeding oncological therapy selection.
  • 4^k Feature Explosion: Tabular $k$-mer representations scale exponentially (256 dims for 4-mers), causing severe sparsity in Euclidean space.
  • Spatial Grammar Collapse: Flat tabular ML models ignore $5' \to 3'$ directional regulatory syntax and promoter context.
  • Homologous Data Leakage: Standard random splits leak related gene family motifs between training and test sets.

🎯 Specific Scope Addressed

  • Zero-Leakage Ingestion: Chromosome-arm partitioning eliminates homologous sequence snooping.
  • Quantum Hilbert Mapping: Parameterized quantum circuits project 346-D feature interactions into 16-D Hilbert space.
  • 1-Based Localizer: Pinpoints exact nucleotide coordinates and classifies transition vs transversion (Ti/Tv).
  • Evidence Anchoring: Directly maps predictions to NCBI ClinVar records and ACMG guidelines without ungrounded clinical claims.

SLIDE 5 :: PROPOSED SYSTEM

Proposed System: Quantum Multi-Modal Fusion (QMFN)

🏛️ Proposed Architecture

  • Tri-Engine Suite: Evaluates 5 Classical Ensembles, 1 Deep Causal TCN, and 2 Quantum Classifiers (VQC, QSVC).
  • Dilated Causal TCN: Exponential receptive field of 63 bp without future token leakage.
  • 4-Qubit PQC: Parameterized quantum circuit with ZZ-feature map and entangling CNOT ring.
  • Kronecker Tensor Fusion: Multiplicative outer product $h_{\text{tcn}} \otimes \langle Z \rangle$ with learned gating $\alpha$.

🚀 Key Contributions & Improvements

  • Empirical Superiority: 96.7% Test Accuracy, 0.990 ROC-AUC (vs TCN 0.985, SVM 0.954).
  • Sub-4ms Inference Latency: 3.80 ms execution time per sequence window.
  • Sharp Calibration: Brier score of 0.082 ensuring reliable probability estimates.
  • Dual Web Deployment: Fully functional via Flask REST server (port 5000) and Streamlit research portal (port 8501).

SLIDE 13 :: PREPROCESSING

Data Preprocessing & 346-D Feature Engineering

🧪 Data Sanitization & Hygiene

  • IUPAC Auditing: Rejection/flagging of degenerate codes (N, R, Y, S) to ensure strict {A, C, G, T} validity.
  • Deduplication: Automated elimination of identical sequences and conflicting multi-label pairs.
  • Quantile Scaling: Outlier suppression with standard $z$-score normalizers fit solely on train splits.
  • Chromosome Isolation: Partitioned by chromosome locus (70% train, 15% val, 15% test) to prevent target snooping.

📊 346-Dimensional Feature Breakdown

  • 16 Dims: 2-mer Dinucleotide Frequency Spectrum ($4^2 = 16$).
  • 64 Dims: 3-mer Trinucleotide Codon Bias Spectrum ($4^3 = 64$).
  • 256 Dims: 4-mer Tetranucleotide Regulatory Motifs ($4^4 = 256$).
  • 10 Dims: Shannon Entropy $H(X)$, Gini-Simpson Diversity, GC Content %, AT Content %, GC-Skew $[(G-C)/(G+C)]$, and length.

SLIDE 14 :: PROPOSED ALGORITHM

Proposed Model: Quantum Multi-Modal Fusion Network

Tensor Kronecker Outer-Product Fusion:
$$z_{\text{fused}} = \text{LayerNorm}\left( W_c h_{\text{tcn}} \oplus W_q \langle Z \rangle \oplus \alpha (h_{\text{tcn}} \otimes \langle Z \rangle) \right)$$
  • Dilated Causal TCN: 4 residual blocks ($d \in \{1,2,4,8\}$, $k=3$) yield a 63-bp receptive field with zero future leakage.
  • 4-Qubit PQC: ZZ-feature map maps non-linear combinations into $2^4 = 16$-D Hilbert space with Pauli-Z measurements $\langle Z \rangle$.
  • Learned Gating ($\alpha$): Dynamically regulates information flow between classical and quantum branches.

SLIDE 22 :: LIMITATIONS

System Limitations & Ethical Boundaries

⚙️ Technical & Hardware Limits

  • Statevector Simulation: Evaluated on classical statevector simulators; physical QPUs introduce gate noise and decoherence.
  • 4-Qubit Register: Qubit register constraint necessitates top-4 PCA reduction of the 346-D feature matrix.
  • Sequence Window: 63-bp receptive field optimized for exons; whole-chromosome reads require sliding windows.
  • In-Silico Callset: Benchmarked on 400 curated variants; requires prospective clinical cohort validation.

⚖️ Ethical & Clinical Boundaries

  • Non-Diagnostic Scope: Academic research software, NOT certified by FDA or CE-IVDR for clinical care.
  • No Patient Decisions: Must not be used for direct patient therapy or surgical planning.
  • Evidence Separation: Statistical model predictions must be verified against certified molecular diagnostics.
  • Curated Grounding: ClinVar annotations depend on current public database curation.
